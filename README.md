# CrimeGraph — AI-Powered Criminal Network Intelligence Platform

> **From fragmented records to connected criminal intelligence.**

CrimeGraph is an investigator-focused intelligence platform designed to transform fragmented crime records into an interactive, explainable criminal-network graph.

It combines **identity resolution, graph analytics, anomaly detection, NLP, and AI-assisted investigation** to help investigators discover hidden relationships between people, communication records, financial activity, vehicles, and other entities.

---

##  Problem

Criminal intelligence is often distributed across multiple sources such as:

- First Information Reports (FIRs)
- Call Detail Records (CDRs)
- Transaction records
- Intelligence reports
- Social-media information
- Vehicle and identity records

Traditional systems primarily store and retrieve these records independently.

The challenge is not simply finding a record — it is understanding:

> **Who is connected to whom, through what evidence, and what role does each individual play within the network?**

CrimeGraph addresses this problem by converting fragmented records into a connected intelligence graph.

---
